Chess Piece Assets

This directory should contain chess piece images in PNG format.
The application supports Unicode character fallback if images are not available.

Recommended Sets (Free/Open Source):
=====================================

1. cburnett (Lichess default)
   - Author: Colin M.L. Burnett
   - License: GPL
   - Source: https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett

2. Wikimedia Commons
   - License: Public Domain / CC0
   - Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces

3. JohnPablok's Improved Pieces
   - Author: JohnPablok
   - License: CC-BY-SA
   - Source: https://opengameart.org/content/chess-pieces-and-board-squares

Required Files:
===============
w_king.png    - White King
w_queen.png   - White Queen
w_rook.png    - White Rook
w_bishop.png  - White Bishop
w_knight.png  - White Knight
w_pawn.png    - White Pawn
b_king.png    - Black King
b_queen.png   - Black Queen
b_rook.png    - Black Rook
b_bishop.png  - Black Bishop
b_knight.png  - Black Knight
b_pawn.png    - Black Pawn

Specifications:
===============
- Format: PNG with alpha transparency
- Size: 60-128 pixels (square)
- Background: Transparent

To Download:
============
Run: python download_assets.py
(Requires: pip install cairosvg)
